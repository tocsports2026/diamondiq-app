1. ATHLETE-VISIBLE VERIFICATION RULE

Change the default rule:

Unverified imported records may be used for INTERNAL OSM research and Admin analysis.

They may NOT be used as factual evidence in an athlete-visible published report unless an OSM Admin explicitly reviews/approves the underlying evidence or report.

Production athlete-visible evidence should therefore be osm_reviewed or cross_verified, unless an Admin explicitly overrides the restriction during report review.

Do not make unverified evidence athlete-visible merely by attaching an "unverified" label.

2. DO NOT USE A UNIVERSAL n=5 CUTOFF

Do not automatically suppress valid historical evidence simply because fewer than 5 records match.

Instead create evidence-strength/sample-size handling.

For example:
- n=0 = DATA GAP / Intelligence Request
- small n = LIMITED SAMPLE, prominently disclosed
- larger n = stronger descriptive evidence

Do not infer statistical significance from these categories.

The exact methodology thresholds should ultimately be configurable by OSM Admin rather than permanently hard-coded.

A report with 3 legitimate historical comparables is preferable to inventing data or pretending no evidence exists. It must simply disclose n=3 and the limitations.

3. MOVE GOOGLE DRIVE INGESTION EARLIER

The existing OSM DiamondIQ research archive already resides in Google Drive. I do not want the production workflow to require repeatedly downloading files locally and manually re-uploading them.

The Drive integration must remain READ ONLY and limited to the specifically shared DiamondIQ research folder.

4. NORMALIZE DRAFT DATA — AVOID DUPLICATE FACTS

Reevaluate whether club_draft_history should be a separate physical table.

If the same historical draft selection already exists in draft_players, club draft history should normally be derived from that canonical record through SQL queries/views.

Do not maintain two independent copies of player/pick/bonus facts that could diverge.

Only create a separate club-specific table for information that genuinely cannot be represented by the canonical draft-selection record.

5. PROVENANCE IS MANDATORY

Keep the proposed row-level provenance and report_citations architecture.

Every factual number, player, selection, bonus, ranking, NIL record, etc. appearing in a report must remain traceable to its underlying production record(s).

6. SOURCE CONFLICTS

Add a design for conflicting source records.

If two legitimate sources disagree about a factual value, DiamondIQ must not silently choose one.

The system should preserve both source records, flag the conflict for OSM review, and allow OSM to designate the approved value/evidence status.

7. RAW SOURCE PRESERVATION

Re-importing an updated spreadsheet must not destroy provenance for reports generated from an earlier version.
