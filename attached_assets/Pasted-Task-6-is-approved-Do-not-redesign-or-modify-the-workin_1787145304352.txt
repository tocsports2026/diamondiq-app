PURPOSE

The undrafted population is a core part of DiamondIQ.

When evaluating a future draft prospect, we need to understand not only players who were drafted, but also comparable players who:

1. were drafted,
2. went undrafted but signed as an NDFA, and
3. went undrafted and have no documented professional signing in our source data.

These outcomes MUST remain distinct.

This step is retrieval integration only.

Do NOT create a new predictive model.
Do NOT create new DiamondIQ inferences.
Do NOT redesign the report system.

--------------------------------------------------
A. PRESERVE OUTCOME DISTINCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------

The retrieval system must distinguish at minimum:

DRAFTED
Player was selected in the MLB Draft.

UNDRAFTED / NDFA SIGNED
Player was not drafted but has a documented NDFA professional signing in our source data.

UNDRAFTED / NO DOCUMENTED SIGNING
Player appears in the 2026 undrafted comparison population and we do not have a documented professional signing for that player.

IMPORTANT:

"No documented signing" does NOT mean the player definitively did not sign.

It means only that no professional signing is documented in the production source data currently available to DiamondIQ.

Do not infer outcomes beyond the source records.
